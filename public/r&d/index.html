Cash share by price band Deed sample · ±25 pts per band

About 6 in 10 $3M+ buyers pay cash (Palm Beach 66%, Broward 68%, Miami-Dade 55%), and the share doesn't rise steadily with price. The most financed tiers are $3–5M (Miami-Dade only 27% cash) and $9–11M (Miami-Dade 29%). The most cash-heavy are $7–9M (77% pooled) and $11–15M (73%).

Most borrowers use banks rather than rate-driven mortgage lenders. Miami-Dade buyers finance more often; Broward leans more on non-bank, hard-money and seller financing.

The more a tier pays cash, the less it moves with rates. Below $1M, three of four buyers finance, and when rates jumped to the 7% range in October those sales fell 12–14%. Above $1M, most buyers pay cash and sales fell only 5%. Above $10M, nearly 9 in 10 pay cash and sales are heading for a record. The $3M+ bands grew 15–37% in the first half even with rates above 6.5%.

06 · Notes

Method, sources and gaps

Method and sources

Unit counts use the Florida Department of Revenue 2026 final NAL tax rolls for Broward (16), Miami-Dade (23) and Palm Beach (60), released October 2026. Each parcel's two most recent sales were kept when the parcel is single-family (use code 001), the sale was improved, the price was $3M or more, and the qualification code was 01, 02 or 03 (arm's length). Six 2026 deeds still pending review (98/99) were included. Multi-parcel deeds were counted once.

Cash shares by band come from samples of county Official Records: a sale counts as cash when no purchase mortgage by the buyer was recorded within about 30 to 65 days of the deed. Each county band is accurate to about ±25 points and pooled bands to about ±15 points; pooled rows weight each county equally. All-price cash shares come from Florida Realtors and MIAMI Realtors MLS reports.

Gaps

2024 vs 2023 by $2M band is missing: the state publishes only the current tax roll, which covers 2025 and 2026 deeds, so the band view compares Jan–Jun 2026 with Jan–Jun 2025.

2026 is cut off at June because Miami-Dade's file only fully covers deeds through June. Broward and Palm Beach run through August: Jan–Aug, Broward $3M+ is up 15.9% (208 → 241) and Palm Beach is up 40.7% (543 → 764).

Deed counts exclude disqualified transfers such as some LLC or related-party deeds and multi-parcel deals, so some trophy sales are missing (the $170M Indian Creek sale does not appear as a qualified single-family deed) and counts don't match MLS totals.

The cash method may slightly overstate cash, since some buyers borrow later, through an entity, or from lenders that don't record in Florida.
